I paint the same handful of trails and treelines, over and over, trying to catch them at a different hour each time.

I trained in painting and printmaking, then spent a decade away from a studio before coming back to it seriously a few years ago. Most work starts outdoors, in front of the subject, and is finished over several sessions in the studio.

I work mostly in oil, occasionally acrylic, on canvas, linen, or panel. Originals can be purchased directly. Studio visits are by appointment, and commissions are occasionally open.
