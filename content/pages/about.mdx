Kristen is an artist who paints moody landscapes and vibrant nature scenes. Her work is about appreciating the beauty and wonder of our natural world. She works from her Michigan studio.

Her work is mostly in acrylic paint on canvas or panel. Originals can be purchased directly. Prints are available for some pieces, and commissions are currently open.
